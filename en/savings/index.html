Savings and compound interest calculator

Estimate your future balance from a starting amount, monthly deposits, annual return and time. The real value strips out inflation so the result is expressed in today's purchasing power.

How this calculation works

Each month the balance is multiplied by (1 + annual return ÷ 12), then the deposit is added at month end. The real value is the final balance divided by (1 + inflation)^years.

Frequently asked questions

Why start early?

Interest earns interest: the longer the horizon, the more compounding outweighs your deposits.

Is the return guaranteed?

No. It is a modelling assumption; real investments fluctuate and can lose value.
