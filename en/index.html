Frequently asked questions

Is my financial data sent anywhere?

No. All calculations run locally in your browser. Only your preferences (language, currency, theme) are stored on your device.

Why not display policy rates directly?

They change with monetary policy committee decisions. For reliable figures we link to the official central bank websites.

Are the results exact?

They are estimates based on standard financial formulas and your assumptions. Your bank or an adviser remains the reference for a precise offer.

Which currencies are available?

TND, DZD, MAD, LYD, EGP, SAR, AED, EUR, USD, GBP, CAD, CHF, XOF, ZAR, NGN and TRY. The choice changes the display only, not the maths.
