About

Last updated: November 2026

Our mission

Savings & Investment Pro is an independent financial-education platform for individuals in the Maghreb, Europe, North America and beyond. Our goal is to make rigorous calculation tools accessible — loan amortization, compound interest, retirement, buy vs rent, time value of money, NPV and IRR, budgeting — alongside guides that explain how rates and inflation affect your purchasing power.

Methodology

  • Standard financial formulas: level annuities, monthly compounding, discounting.
  • Policy rates: we link to official central bank websites rather than republishing values that change.
  • Calculations are tested against independently computed results.

Publisher and independence

Publisher: Haykel Laouiti, engineer and writer specializing in analytical tools and financial modelling — La Marsa, Tunis, Tunisia.

The site receives no commission from banks, which keeps the calculators independent. It is funded by advertising (Google AdSense).

Disclaimer

No content is personalized investment advice. Consult your adviser or bank before any decision.
