How to reduce the cost of a loan

Last updated: November 2026

Nominal rate, APR, term, insurance and early repayment: practical levers to pay less for your loan.

Compare on APR, not just the rate

Two offers with the same nominal rate can cost very different amounts once fees, insurance and guarantees are added. The APR bundles these into a single annual percentage and is the figure to compare. Always ask for the full schedule and total cost of credit, not just the monthly payment.

Play with the term

A longer term lowers the monthly payment but sharply raises the total cost, because interest accrues for longer on a slowly shrinking balance. A shorter term costs more each month but saves interest. Find the highest payment your budget can bear without weakening your emergency savings.

Repay early

Every extra payment reduces the outstanding balance and therefore the interest of the following months. The earlier it happens the bigger the saving, since the first years are mostly interest. Check for early-repayment penalties and test the effect in our loan calculator before committing.

Negotiate insurance and fees

Borrower insurance can weigh heavily over the life of a loan. Compare outside offers where local rules allow it, and negotiate origination fees: they are rarely fixed.
